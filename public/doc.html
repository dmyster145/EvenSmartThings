Getting Started

Welcome

Control your SmartThings scenes and devices from your G2 glasses. Run scenes with one tap, or open a room to switch and dim devices.

Setup

Open this app in the Even App on your phone and tap Connect SmartThings. The app uses SmartThings OAuth instead of a manual token field. After you authorize, the backend keeps the SmartThings refresh token and renews short-lived access tokens as needed. To reconnect or disconnect later, scroll to the SmartThings connection section in the configuration panel (above Documentation).

On the glasses

You'll see a main menu: Scenes, Devices, Favorites (if you've added any), and Tap to speak for hands-free voice control. In the configuration panel you can set the default launch view, Home screen order (including where Tap to speak appears), list order for each list, favorites, stats visibility (toggles), and custom names. Scroll to choose, tap to open or run. Scrolling past the top or bottom of any list wraps around to the other end.

Launch Default

Use Default view in the App Launch section of the configuration panel to choose what opens first whenever this app launches. You can pick Resume last screen, Main screen, Scenes, Devices, or Favorites.

Resume last screen is the fastest option if you tend to return to the same room, device, or favorites list. If you choose Favorites but you do not have any favorites yet, the app falls back to Scenes. This launch preference is honored whether you open the app from a QR code, the phone companion, or the glasses.

Scenes

Tap Scenes on the main menu to see your SmartThings scenes. Scroll to highlight one, tap to run it. In the configuration panel, List order sets sort order per list (Home screen, Scenes, Rooms, Devices, Favorites): Alphabetical, Reverse, or Custom. For Custom: choose Custom from the dropdown, select an item, use ↑ Up and ↓ Down to reorder, then Done.

Devices & Rooms

Tap Devices to see your rooms. Pick a room, then scroll through its devices. Tap a device for On/Off and (if supported) Dim. Tap All to turn all devices in the room on or off, or set a dim level for all dimmable devices.

Double-tap from a list to go back (e.g. from devices to rooms, or from a room to the main menu).

Favorites

Favorites is a single list that can mix scenes and devices. In the configuration panel: Add to Favorites → pick scenes and/or devices → Add selected (repeat as needed) → Done. Use Remove to take an item off the list. On the glasses, tap Favorites, then scroll and tap to run a scene or open a device. Order can be alphabetical, reverse, or custom.

Voice (Tap to speak)

How it works

Tap to speak is a main-menu item that lets you run scenes, switch devices, or open a room by voice. Tap it once and start speaking right away. Speech recognition runs entirely on the glasses (offline); nothing is sent to the cloud.

Using it

  1. From the main menu, tap Tap to speak. The right panel shows Listening… and then what it heard.
  2. Say a command, for example: "Run Movie Night", "Turn off kitchen lights", "Lab lights off", or "Open garage".
  3. The matched command is shown for confirmation, e.g. Run "Movie Night"? Tap to confirm · double-tap cancel.
  4. Tap once to confirm and run it. Double-tap to cancel.

Phrasing

  • Scenes: "run", "activate", or just the scene name. A scene named for an action (e.g. Lab: OFF) is matched even when you say "lab lights off".
  • Devices on/off: "turn on/off <device>"; a bare device name turns it on.
  • Dim / brightness: "set <device> to 20 percent", "turn <device> to twenty percent", "dim <device> to 50" (dimmable devices only).
  • Doors & shades: "open/close <garage door>", "open/close <blinds>", "set <blinds> to 40 percent".
  • Climate & fans: "<bulb> warmer/cooler", "<fan> faster/slower", "<thermostat> warmer/cooler", "set <thermostat> to heat/cool/auto".
  • Locks: "lock/unlock <door>" (also "secure/unsecure").
  • Buttons: "press/push <button>".
  • Media: "play/pause/resume/stop <speaker>", "mute/unmute <speaker>", "<speaker> next/previous/skip/rewind", "volume up/down <speaker>", "<speaker> louder/quieter".
  • Rooms: "<room> on/off" switches every switchable device in that room; "<room> to 30 percent" dims every dimmable device in it; "open <room>" / "go to <room>" opens its device list.
  • Duplicates: if several devices share a name (e.g. six "Kitchen Lights"), the command is applied to all of them together.
  • Each command is routed only to a device that actually supports it, so an unsupported phrase never fires the wrong action. Filler words are ignored and minor mis-hearing is tolerated; if nothing is close enough it shows No match — tap to try again (no confirmation needed) — just tap to retry.
Tip: A command is never run until you confirm it with a tap, so a misheard phrase can't trigger the wrong scene or device. Use the configuration panel's Home screen order to move Tap to speak to the top for fastest access.
Stats & Visibility

On the right side of the glasses display you see status info: global device counts, room device counts, or per-device details (type, protocol, on/off, brightness, and capability readings). In the configuration panel, Stats visibility uses toggle switches to show or hide each stat. The All toggle at the top turns the entire stats section on or off—when off, the section is hidden completely, including the SmartThings / room / device header. Use it for a cleaner list-only view.

Custom names

Give devices, rooms, or scenes a display name used only in this app. In the configuration panel: Add custom name → choose Type (Scene, Room, or Device), pick the item, enter the name → Save. Add more if you like, then Done to close. Entries show as SmartThings name > your name (e.g. ALL Lamps: OFF > Test lamps off). Stored locally; SmartThings is unchanged. Clear removes a custom name.

Controls

Four gestures on your G2 touchpad:

Scroll Swipe up or down to move the highlight. Past the top or bottom it wraps around to the other end.
Tap Confirm: run a scene, open a device, choose an option, or confirm a voice command.
Double Tap Go back one level, or cancel a pending voice command.
Tip: Double-tap from the main menu to go back to the previous view if you navigated from another app. After a voice command is recognized, a single tap confirms it and a double-tap cancels it.
Tips
  • List order, favorites, stats toggles, and custom names are set in the configuration panel; changes apply on the glasses immediately.
  • Favorites can include scenes and devices; tap a scene to run it, a device to open its controls. Done closes the picker.
  • Custom names apply only in this app; SmartThings and automations keep the original names. List format: SmartThings name > your name.
  • Tap to speak runs offline on the glasses and always asks you to confirm with a tap before acting, a misheard phrase never fires the wrong command. Reorder it on the Home screen in the configuration panel.
  • Scrolling wraps around at the ends of every list, so the last item is one swipe up from the first.
